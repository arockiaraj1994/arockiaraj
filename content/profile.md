I'm a Development Manager at Baton Systems. I've been building software since 2014, and the last 7 years have been in FinTech, mostly on FX and collateral management: building configurable products that teams can reuse and automating the repetitive parts.

Outside work, I was on the CFP and mentorship team for PyCon India 2024 and 2025, and I speak at Chennaipy. I like system design, enterprise integration patterns, and no-code and low-code tools.

In my spare time I build small apps with AI coding tools, including an Android app on Google Play, and write about what I learn. See my [projects](/projects/), [posts](/posts/) and [Chennaipy talks](/talks/).
